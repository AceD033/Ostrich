FAQ

Common questions about privacy, access and using Ostrich.

Is Ostrich really anonymous?

Yes. Ostrich is built to collect as little personal data as possible and keeps your conversations private by default.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You can start chatting without a phone number or a long sign-up.

Can I use it in a browser?

Yes. The web version works in desktop and mobile browsers.

Is there a mobile or desktop app?

Not yet. Apps for every platform are in development. Until then, use the web version in any browser.

How do I contact support?

See the Support page for common fixes, or email support@ostrich-messenger.com.

What makes Ostrich different?

It puts privacy and speed first and keeps the interface clean, without extras you don’t need.
