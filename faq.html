FAQ

Here you can find the most common questions about using Ostrich, privacy, access, and the app experience.

Is Ostrich really anonymous?

Yes. The platform is designed to minimize personal data and keep communication private by default.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You can use the service without a personal phone number or long registration process.

Can I use it from a browser?

Yes. The web version is available on desktop and mobile browsers with quick access.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. The app is available for Android devices and designed to work simply and quickly.

Can I contact support?

Yes. If you need help, go to the Support page and send a request.

What makes Ostrich different?

It focuses on privacy, speed, and a clean interface without unnecessary extras.
