Support

Answers about privacy, access and everyday use, and how to reach us if you’re stuck.

Privacy

You don’t need a phone number or personal data to start chatting. Your messages stay private.

Getting started

Open Ostrich, start a chat and send your first message. There’s no registration form.

Troubleshooting

If the app doesn’t load or a message doesn’t send, check your connection, refresh the page and try again.

Still stuck?

Write to support@ostrich-messenger.com and we’ll reply by email.
