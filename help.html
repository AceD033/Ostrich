Support

Need help with privacy, app access, or using the messenger? This page contains the main answers, tips, and contact details to make your experience simple and safe.

🔒 Privacy

We do not require a phone number or personal data to start chatting. Your messages stay private and anonymous.

⚡ Fast use

Open the app, start a chat, and begin messaging immediately. No complicated registration is required.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the app does not load or messages do not send, refresh the page, check the connection, and try again.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ostrich really anonymous?

Yes. The platform is designed to avoid personal identification and keep the interaction minimal and private.

Can I use it on the web?

Yes. The web version is available from any browser and works quickly on desktop and mobile devices.

Do I need to register?

Usually not. The main goal is to keep access quick and without unnecessary personal data.
